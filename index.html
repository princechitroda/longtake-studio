The approach

A long take is one unbroken shot — the camera keeps running, nothing cuts. That's the site, too.

3industries served
1founder, every project
∞revisions until it's right*
IN → 🌍built in India, shipped worldwide

Longtake is a small, senior studio — you work directly with the person building your site, not an account manager passing your brief down a chain.

Most sites are built as a stack of static sections. We build them as one continuous sequence: scroll becomes the camera move, and every section is a scene in the same shot rather than a new page loading in.

That takes longer to build than a template. It's also the entire reason to hire a studio instead of dragging blocks around a website builder.

*within the agreed scope of the project — see terms on request.
