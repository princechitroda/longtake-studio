The approach

A long take is one unbroken shot — the camera keeps running, nothing cuts. That's the site, too.

3industries shipped in
1founder on every project, start to finish
35years of client history rebuilt
0templates used

Longtake is a small, senior studio. You work directly with the person building your site — not an account manager passing your brief down a chain.

Most sites are built as a stack of static sections. We build them as one continuous sequence: scroll becomes the camera move, and each section is a scene in the same shot rather than a new page loading in.

That takes longer to build than a template. It is also the entire reason to hire a studio instead of dragging blocks around a website builder.
